Common questions

Why does my St. Cloud well pump sand?

Sand usually means the well screen or casing is failing, or the pump sits too low. It destroys pumps quickly, so it should be diagnosed before another pump is installed.

Do St. Cloud well repairs need permits?

Pump and tank swaps are generally maintenance, while new wells and major well work need permits. Your licensed contractor confirms what applies to your job.
