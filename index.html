Common questions

How much does well pump replacement cost in Central Florida?

Most homeowners pay around $1,900 for a standard submersible pump replacement, with simple jobs below that and deep wells or tank-and-pump jobs above it. Your quote should itemize pump, labor, and any tank or switch work.

How long does a well pump last in Florida?

Most submersible well pumps last 10 to 15 years in Florida. Heat, sand, and frequent cycling in irrigation-heavy homes can shorten that.
