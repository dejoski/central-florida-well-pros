Well Repair Services in Central Florida

Well repair starts with one question: is the problem the pump, the tank, the controls, or the well itself? Each has a different price, and guessing wrong is how homeowners buy a pump when they needed a switch.

Central Florida's common repair list is short and knowable: waterlogged pressure tanks, failed pressure switches, worn control boxes, corroded fittings in sandy soil, and pumps at end of life. Describe your symptoms once and the contractor matched to you starts from that diagnosis — not from zero at your door.

What we handle

  • No-water troubleshooting by symptom
  • Pressure tank and switch replacement
  • Control box and electrical faults
  • Sand and water-quality problems traced to the well

Common questions

Why does my well pump keep cycling on and off?

Short-cycling almost always means a waterlogged pressure tank or a tank with wrong air charge. It will kill a good pump if ignored, so fix the tank first.

Is well repair covered by homeowners insurance?

Sudden failures sometimes are; wear-and-tear usually is not. A written diagnosis from a licensed contractor is what insurers ask for either way.
