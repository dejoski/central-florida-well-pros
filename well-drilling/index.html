Well Drilling in Central Florida: New Wells Done Right

Drilling a well in Central Florida means drilling into one of the most productive aquifer systems in the country — but the paperwork matters as much as the rig. New wells need permits, setbacks from septic and property lines, casing, grouting, and disinfection before the first glass of water.

Licensed Florida drillers handle that chain end to end. Whether it is a household well for a new build on a fringe lot, a dedicated irrigation well to get a big lot off city water bills, or a replacement well for a failing one, the quote should name depth, casing, pump, and tank — not just a per-foot number.

What we handle

  • Household wells for new builds and off-grid lots
  • Dedicated irrigation wells
  • Replacement wells for failed casing or low yield
  • Permit, casing, and disinfection included by the driller

Common questions

How much does it cost to drill a well in Central Florida?

Total cost depends on depth, casing, and the pump package. Get itemized quotes — drilling, casing, pump, tank, and permits as separate lines.

Do I need a permit to drill a well in Florida?

Yes. Water management district and county rules apply, and licensed drillers pull the permits as part of the job.
